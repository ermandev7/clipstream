Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

ClipStream ("the app") is a desktop application for Windows provided by the ClipStream project ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

1. Data processed on your computer

The videos, streams and files you add, their transcriptions, the clips the app generates and their titles and settings are processed and stored only on your computer. We do not operate servers that receive this data.

2. TikTok account data

If you choose to connect a TikTok account, the app uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API:

  • What we receive from TikTok: an access token and a refresh token, your account's open ID, and basic profile information (display name, username and avatar) together with your posting options (available privacy levels, interaction settings and maximum video duration).
  • Why: to show you which account a clip will be posted to and to upload the clips you select and confirm.
  • Where it is stored: tokens are stored on your computer, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) so only your Windows user can read them. They are never sent to us or to any third party other than TikTok.
  • What is sent to TikTok: only the video file and the post settings (text, privacy level, interaction settings, commercial content disclosure) of each clip you confirm, at the moment you press Publish or at the time you scheduled.
  • How to revoke: press "Disconnect" in the app (this revokes the token with TikTok and deletes it from your computer), or remove ClipStream from your TikTok account's app permissions.

3. Other services

To download a video you provide a link to (for example from YouTube or Kick), the app connects to that service from your computer. AI models used for analysis run locally on your PC.

4. Analytics and advertising

The app has no analytics, tracking or advertising.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country to use TikTok).

6. Your rights

Because data stays on your computer, you can delete it at any time by deleting the clips in the app or uninstalling it and removing the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\ClipStream. For questions, contact us at dragonazul875@gmail.com.

7. Changes

We will publish any changes to this policy on this page with a new date.
