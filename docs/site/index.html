Your streams, as vertical clips.

ClipStream is a Windows desktop app for live streamers and video creators. It analyses your own streams and videos on your PC, finds the best moments, and cuts them into 9:16 clips with subtitles, ready for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

How it works

  1. Add a source. A finished stream, a published video of yours, or a file on your PC.
  2. Automatic analysis. Speech, audio and chat activity are analysed locally; you see every step with its progress.
  3. Review and edit. Trim, change the framing and subtitles, and adjust the title and hashtags of each clip.
  4. Publish. Connect your TikTok account and publish each clip after reviewing it: you choose who can see it, interactions and commercial content disclosure. Nothing is uploaded until you press Publish.

Privacy first

Video analysis runs entirely on your computer. ClipStream has no servers and no user accounts of its own. Your TikTok access is stored encrypted on your PC and you can disconnect at any time.
